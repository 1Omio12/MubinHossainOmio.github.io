BSc Thesis

VVER-1200 Spent Fuel Dry Cask Storage

Multiphysics Safety Analysis

A full-chain computational safety case for VVER-1200 spent fuel dry cask storage: neutronics and shielding in OpenMC, thermal-hydraulics in Ansys, and off-site atmospheric dispersion consequence modeling in HotSpot.

OpenMC Monte Carlo Analysis

Subsection 1

Pin-Level Fuel Cell Depletion Analysis

Axial burnup zoning of the VVER-1200 hexagonal fuel pin cell — UO₂ fuel (4.5% enr.), He gap, E110 Zr cladding, and borated moderator, discretized into 10 axial zones (Z1–Z10) for depletion tracking.

Subsection 2

Criticality Analysis

Full cask k-eff evaluation for 5-year-cooled spent fuel — radial (XY) basket/shell/concrete layout and axial (XZ) profile including helium backfill, plenum, and steel caps.

Subsection 3

Flood Analysis

Reactivity response under a hypothetical flooding accident scenario — unborated flood water ingress replacing the normal borated/inert environment around the spent fuel basket.

Subsection 4

Radiation (Shielding & Dose) Analysis

XZ dose-rate map across the full cask cross-section, annotated with fuel, helium, steel, air-gap, and concrete regions — identifying the peak surface dose rate location.

Ansys Thermal-Hydraulics

Cask Geometry & Steady-State Temperature Field

Axial cross-sectional cutaway and full 3D CAD model of the dry cask, plus the resulting steady-state static temperature distribution from CFD thermal simulation.

Atmospheric Dispersion Analysis (HotSpot)

Plume Contour Modeling

Off-site consequence modeling using HotSpot General Plume — Total Effective Dose (TED, Sv) contour under Stability Class D conditions.

Ongoing Project

Digital Twin — Spent Fuel Pool

IoT-Based Real-Time Thermal Monitoring

An experimental helical heat-exchanger rig — a controlled heater standing in for the spent fuel assembly — instrumented and mirrored by an IoT-based Digital Twin. Live thermal readings are validated against theoretical, experimental, and Ansys simulation results, with a machine-learning layer that reconstructs readings for any sensor that fails.

Physical & Experimental Setup

Subsection 1

Full CAD Model

Complete 3D assembly of the helical coil heat exchanger loop — hot and cold reservoirs, pumps, and piping — used as the basis for both the physical rig and the digital twin's Ansys reference model.

Subsection 2

Experimental Bench Rig

The physically built rig — controlled heater as dummy fuel, hot/cold reservoirs, dual pumps, and instrumented piping — generating the live thermal data the digital twin mirrors.

HX·DT — Live Twin Dashboard

3D Twin, Hardware Controls & Live Sensors

The custom HX·DT interface renders the heat exchanger loop in interactive 3D alongside real-time pump/heater controls and a live sensor grid — hot/cold inlet & outlet temperatures, flow rates, and derived thermal performance metrics (heat transfer rate Q, LMTD, U-coefficient, ΔT, and heat transfer area), streamed straight from the rig's hardware.

Machine Learning — Virtual Sensor Reconstruction

Best Virtual Sensor Performance per Failed Sensor

When a physical sensor drops out, trained ML models reconstruct its expected reading from the remaining live sensors — keeping the twin's thermal picture complete even under hardware failure.

Flagship Project

RADSAFE-IoT

Real-Time Radiation Monitoring & Emergency Response

A national award-winning real-time radiation monitoring system — ESP32 & Geiger-Müller counters feeding a live mapping dashboard, geospatial ML for interpolation and anomaly detection, and an AI-powered evacuation route planner that reacts to live radiation heatmaps.

Development Timeline

Five Hardware Revisions, 2024 – 2025

From a bare radiation-only prototype to an AI-powered handheld: v0.1 introduced basic detection, v0.2 added GPS connectivity, v1.0 brought a touch-screen interface, v1.1 fused multiple sensors, and v2.0 shipped as the AI-powered RADSAFE unit.

Live Radiation Mapping Dashboard

Circle Marker Visualization with Statistical Analysis

Live web dashboard plotting every field reading as a color-coded circle marker across the MIST campus, with a legend spanning Normal, Action, Warning, and Emergency radiation levels (μSv/h) and one-click access to the latest logged location.

Geospatial Data & ML Analysis

Spatial Distribution of Radiation Measurements — MIST & INST

Side-by-side spatial scatter plots of collected radiation readings across two survey sites (MIST and the Institute of Nuclear Science & Technology), color-mapped by dose rate (μSv/h) to reveal hotspot clustering used for downstream geospatial interpolation and anomaly detection models. A continuous contour interpolation over the MIST survey area further resolves the discrete readings into a smooth radiation field, sharpening the hotspot near the reactor building.

AI-Powered Emergency Evacuation Routing

Enhanced Escape Route Map over a Live Radiation Heatmap

Route-planning engine overlaying a generated radiation heatmap (Very Low to Extreme, μSv/h) on satellite imagery, then computing the safest path between two points — steering evacuees around high-dose zones instead of straight-line routing.

Active Research

Heavy Metal Contamination & Radiation Survey

Mirpur Cantonment Firing Range, Dhaka

Military firing ranges accumulate heavy metals — lead, copper, zinc, nickel, iron — from repeated bullet discharge. This study tested a handheld XRF spectrometer (Evident Vanta, Alloy Plus mode) as a rapid in-situ screening tool for that contamination, paired with an IoT radiation detector for ambient dose monitoring across 25 surveyed shooting spots.

Survey Design

25-Point In-Situ Sampling Grid

Georeferenced survey layout across the Mirpur Cantonment firing range, tracing the shooting lane from the firing positions down to the impact zone — each point (S1–S25) hXRF-scanned and radiation-logged in place.

Field Methodology

Dual Handheld Instrumentation

Each survey point was measured with both instruments side by side — the hXRF spectrometer for elemental composition (validated against five certified reference materials) and the custom RADSAFE IoT detector, GPS-tagged, for ambient radiation dose.

Geospatial Contamination Mapping

Lead Interpolation — IDW vs. Ordinary Kriging

Two spatial interpolation methods applied to the surveyed Pb concentrations, both converging on the same hotspot near the firing positions — concentrations there exceeded 20% Pb, over 500× the EU's 400 ppm residential soil guideline, while values dropped toward background near the impact zone.

The results confirm hXRF Alloy Plus mode as a viable first-pass screening tool, and flag the range for Pb remediation.

Startup · CTO

LEAFY

Smart Rooftop Hydroponics for Urban Bangladesh

LEAFY is an AgriTech and climate-tech startup turning unused urban rooftops into high-yield, smart hydroponic farms. With Bangladesh losing over 69,000 hectares of arable land annually to urbanization and facing mounting water scarcity, LEAFY uses proprietary IoT automation and AI to decentralize food production — putting turnkey hydroponic systems in the hands of urban households, housewives, and young entrepreneurs, and supplying fresh, pesticide-free produce directly to urban retailers. As CTO, I lead technology strategy and product development for the platform.

System Architecture

NFT Hydroponic Loop with AI-Driven IoT Control

A closed-loop Nutrient Film Technique (NFT) grow bed — shade net and rain cover up top, root-zone film and NFT trays for the crop, a nutrient reservoir with submersible pump below, and an AI-driven IoT controller that runs dosing pumps and reads pH, EC, temperature, and humidity in real time.

In-House IoT Controller

Custom-Built Dosing & Monitoring Unit

LEAFY R&D's own controller hardware — touchscreen readout, physical controls, and dosing-pump drivers housed in a rugged enclosure — built to run unattended on a rooftop through heat, humidity, and rain.
